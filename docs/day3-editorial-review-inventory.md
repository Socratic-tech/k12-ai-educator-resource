## Discipline-authenticity findings

- Physical Education explicitly addresses movement objectives, equipment, space, group size, physical and emotional safety, skill assessment, and accessibility. No vocabulary-swapped generic examples were identified.
- Visual Arts distinguishes artistic process from product and directly addresses critique, authorship, copyright, generative imagery, material choices, and ownership uncertainty. Human review remains essential because copyright interpretations evolve.
- Music distinguishes general music, band, choir, and orchestra; examples address rehearsal, ensemble technique, theory, composition, pronunciation, performance, and authorship. Repertoire, translation, and technical claims require specialist review.
- World Languages centers proficiency, communicative modes, authentic production, translation limits, regional variation, culture, speaking, and writing practice. These claims require review by educators familiar with the named languages and communities.
- Elementary centers adult mediation, developmental appropriateness, concrete learning, young learners, family communication, and privacy.
- Secondary Core explicitly separates ELA, mathematics, science, and social studies practices. It does not present the four disciplines as interchangeable, but each discipline should receive its own reviewer.

## Editorial flags requiring human action

1. Validate copyright and ownership framing in Visual Arts, Music, the 20-minute guide, and Students + AI with qualified policy/legal reviewers.
2. Validate Music pronunciation, translation, ranges, transpositions, repertoire context, and ensemble distinctions with music educators.
3. Validate World Languages proficiency, naturalness, regional variation, translation, and cultural examples with language educators and represented communities.
4. Review Secondary Core separately with ELA, mathematics, science, and social studies specialists.
5. Review AI-detection language and academic-integrity process with current local policy; retain the principle that detector scores are not definitive proof.
6. Review Elementary and Students + AI for age/developmental appropriateness and approved-tool expectations.
7. Supply a real, monitored feedback destination before broader launch; the current copy-based mechanism is functional but intentionally does not collect data.
